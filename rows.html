SyRI welfare fraud-detection system halted by court

Case · NL — Netherlands · 2020-02-05

The District Court of The Hague ruled the SyRI legislation (Systeem Risico Indicatie) unlawful for breaching Article 8 ECHR: the opaque, inter-agency data-linkage risk model targeted low-income neighbourhoods and lacked sufficient safeguards, with a risk of discrimination by socio-economic and migration background. The system was halted.

Source: UN OHCHR, 5 Feb 2020

Dutch childcare benefits scandal (Toeslagenaffaire) — nationality-based risk profiling

Case · NL — Netherlands · 2020-12-17

The parliamentary inquiry report 'Ongekend onrecht' (17 December 2020) found the Tax Administration's childcare-benefit fraud approach used a risk model that included a self-learning algorithm with (dual) nationality as an indicator. Around 26,000 families were wrongly accused of fraud and faced full recovery of benefits; the approach ran from about 2005 to 2019, and the cabinet resigned on 15 January 2021.

Source: Tweede Kamer, Kamerstukken II 2020/21, 35 510, nr. 2 — 'Ongekend onrecht'
