SyRI welfare fraud-detection system halted by court

Case · NL — Netherlands · 2020-02-05

The District Court of The Hague ruled the SyRI legislation (Systeem Risico Indicatie) unlawful for breaching Article 8 ECHR: the opaque, inter-agency data-linkage risk model targeted low-income neighbourhoods and lacked sufficient safeguards, with a risk of discrimination by socio-economic and migration background. The system was halted.

Source: UN OHCHR, 5 Feb 2020

ChatGPT data breach exposing users' conversations and payment data

Case · IT — Italy · 2023-03-20

On 20 March 2023 a ChatGPT bug exposed other users' chat histories and partial payment details; the breach was cited by the Garante in its 31 March 2023 action against OpenAI, alongside the absence of a legal basis for training-data collection and of age verification.

Official response · 2022-10-17 · binding: yes

CNIL's restricted committee imposed the then-maximum GDPR fine of €20m on Clearview AI for unlawful biometric processing, failure to honour access/erasure rights and non-cooperation, ordered it to stop collecting French residents' data and delete what it held within two months, under a €100,000/day penalty. Announced 20 October 2022.

Source: CNIL, Deliberation SAN-2022-019 of 17 Oct 2022 (Légifrance)

Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens — enforcement

Official response · 2022-04-07 · binding: yes

The Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens fined the Minister of Finance/Tax Administration €3.7m for the Fraude Signalering Voorziening (FSV), a blacklist holding fraud signals on over 270,000 people (2013–2020) with no legal basis, inaccurate/outdated data, excessive retention and inadequate security. Digital Omnibus on AI — Regulation (EU) 2026/1744

Policy · EU — European Union (EU-wide) · 2026-07-08 · in force

Amends the AI Act to simplify implementation: postpones high-risk application dates (2 Dec 2027 for stand-alone Annex III systems; 2 Aug 2028 for Annex I product-embedded systems), narrows some duties, and adds two new prohibited practices (Art. 5(1)(ba)–(bb)): AI systems generating non-consensual intimate material and child sexual abuse material, applicable from 2 Dec 2026. Published in the OJ 24 July 2026, in force 27 July 2026.

Source: EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744

Policy · IT — Italy · 2025-09-23 · in force

Italy's comprehensive national AI law ("Disposizioni e deleghe al Governo in materia di intelligenza artificiale"), the first such law in the EU; it sets a biennial national AI strategy, designates AgID and the National Cybersecurity Agency (ACN) as national AI authorities, and adds AI-related criminal offences.
